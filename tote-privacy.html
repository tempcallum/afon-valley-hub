AFON VALLEY AFC

Weekly Tote - Privacy Notice

Committee-approved Privacy Notice (v1.1). Adopted by the Afon Valley FC committee on 13 October 2026. Public ticket sales remain closed.

Who is responsible for your data?

Afon Valley Football Club (registered for lottery purposes as Afon Valley AFC) is the data controller. Contact afonvalleyafc@gmail.com. Correspondence address: De Havilland Road, Newport, NP10 9PS. The Tote is registered with Newport City Council under number 26/02939/LOTTER.

What information do we collect?

  • Your name, email address, optional mobile number and confirmation that you are at least 16.
  • Ticket count, ticket numbers, draw details, referral source and purchase history.
  • Your Lloyds bank-transfer payment reference, expected and received amount, recorded receipt time, verification result and relevant refund details. We do not collect full card details through the Tote.
  • Winner details, prize amounts, verified payout reference notes, complaints and required lottery audit records.
  • Limited technical information used to detect duplicate or suspicious orders.

Why do we use it?

We use entry and contact details to fulfil the ticket transaction, verify payment, send tickets, conduct the draw and contact winners (contractual necessity). We keep required financial, lottery and regulatory records to comply with legal obligations. We investigate mismatches, disputes or suspected fraud as part of our legitimate interests in running a fair and secure Tote.

We do not collect Tote email addresses for unrelated marketing. Any separate marketing communications require an appropriate lawful basis.

Who may access or receive your information?

Only authorised club officials should access the information they need. The Hub uses Supabase for its application and data services; the club uses Google Gmail and Apps Script to send operational emails. Lloyds holds the relevant bank-transfer records. Relevant service providers may process information on the club's behalf. Where required, the club may disclose information to Newport City Council, the Gambling Commission, police or other competent authorities.

How long is it kept?

We retain records as necessary for lottery reporting, financial accountability, dispute handling and other applicable legal obligations, then securely delete or anonymise information no longer needed. Some providers may handle information outside the UK, subject to applicable data-transfer safeguards.

What information about winners is public?

We normally minimise public winner information, such as showing a first name and surname initial, unless the winner agrees to more or another lawful reason applies. Additional details may be needed privately to arrange payment.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and statutory record-keeping obligations, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, or object to some processing. Email afonvalleyafc@gmail.com with a request. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates

We will update this notice if our operation or handling of personal data materially changes. The club will publish an updated approved notice if material changes are needed.
