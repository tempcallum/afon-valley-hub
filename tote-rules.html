Weekly Tote — rules and information

Lottery terms updated 19 October 2026. The existing Draw 3 entrants have agreed to the improved prize allocation. The initial committee rules were adopted on 13 October 2026. Draw times and existing ticket numbers remain unchanged.

Who runs the Tote?

The Afon Valley AFC Tote is promoted by Afon Valley AFC to support the club's non-commercial sporting purposes. The society is registered with Newport City Council as a small society lottery, registration 26/02939/LOTTER.

Who can enter?

You must be 16 or over. You must provide a name, a valid email address and confirm that you meet the minimum age requirement.

Ticket price and how to enter

Each entry costs £1. The public checkout lets you choose 1, 5, 10 or 20 entries in a purchase. All tickets in the same draw have the same price.

For one-off purchases, you receive bank-transfer instructions and a payment reference unique to that order. Pressing “I've paid” is not proof of payment. Tickets are only allocated after the club verifies the correct payment reached its Lloyds account by the published cutoff. Late, incorrect or unmatched payments require manual review and may not be entered into that draw.

Optional weekly standing orders

You may instead register for 1, 5, 10 or 20 tickets a week and set up a weekly standing order in your own banking app, preferably for Monday. Your permanent bank reference identifies your recurring subscription; a different ticket number is issued for each paid draw. The bank standing order is not established automatically by the Hub.

The club checks each payment received and issues tickets only when the amount and permanent reference match and the current draw is open before its published sales cutoff. The ticket/order identifier printed for a particular draw is separate from the permanent bank reference. If payment is delayed, missing, duplicated, incomplete, paid using a wrong reference, or received after sales close, tickets are not issued automatically and the club will review or refund the payment as appropriate. There is no guarantee of a ticket every week without verified payment.

A supporter can stop future entries using their private Tote management link or by contacting the club, but must also cancel the standing order directly with their bank to stop payments. Already issued tickets remain valid. Keep your private management link secure and check your email for weekly ticket confirmations. Standing orders are optional; one-off purchases remain available.

When does the draw take place?

The normal draw is Friday at 8pm UK time. Entries close at 7:55pm. The exact date and cutoff for the relevant draw are shown on the entry screen and ticket confirmation. A postponed draw will be communicated to ticket holders.

How are winners chosen?

  • Jackpot: one number is drawn from the pool of 500 possible numbers (1 to 500). If a paid entry holds that number, it wins the accumulated jackpot. If no paid entry holds it, the jackpot rolls over to a later Tote draw.
  • Weekly prize: one ticket is drawn from the tickets actually sold and paid for that week. If there are no paid entries, there is no weekly winner.
  • A single ticket could win both prizes if selected in both draws.

The system uses a random seed committed before the draw and reveals it after the draw so the selection can be audited. Prize amounts depend on actual paid sales and any jackpot brought forward, not on advertised examples.

How the ticket money is used

For Draw 3 (23 October 2026) and subsequent draws until revised with notice, the allocation of ticket proceeds is 25% for Afon Valley AFC's purposes, 45% to the rolling jackpot and 30% to one weekly winning ticket. The one weekly winner is selected from paid tickets for that draw. Prize amounts depend on paid sales, and a supporter is not guaranteed to win. Minor rounding differences are accounted for by the draw system. At least 20% of each draw’s proceeds must be applied to the club’s purposes. Lottery administration expenses are covered separately from the prize allocations.

Prize limits and paying winners

The maximum value of any single small society lottery prize is £25,000. The club contacts winners using the details supplied with the paid entry. Prize payments are arranged and verified by club officials; a winning notification itself is not a bank transfer. If you believe you have won and have not been contacted, email the club.

Privacy information

You can read the Afon Valley Tote Privacy Notice separately. These rules were approved by the committee. Live entries are accepted only through the official checkout while a draw is open and the payment service is available.

Help, complaints and data

For ticket queries, payment issues, privacy requests, or complaints, contact afonvalleyafc@gmail.com. The club uses the name and contact details you provide to manage entries, confirm payment, issue ticket records, contact winners and maintain required lottery records. See the Tote Privacy Notice for the complete privacy information.

Keeping a copy

You may save or print this page for reference. Your individual electronic ticket confirmation records your draw, paid amount, ticket numbers and organiser information.
